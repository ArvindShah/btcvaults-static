Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is btcvaults?

A: btcvaults is a collaborative custody platform for the B2B market, providing Bitcoin custody infrastructure for professionals. We enable secure multisig vaults, lending with overcollateralized Bitcoin, and ASX-listed access to 100% BTC on balance sheet.

Q: How does the Multisignature Vault platform work?

A: Our Vaults Platform™ is a B2B multisig solution allowing Bitcoin Advisors to create and manage collaborative custody vaults for clients (SMSFs, family offices, companies) while retaining client key majority. This requires multiple keys to authorize transactions, enhancing security.

Q: What are Lending Vaults™?

A: Lending Vaults™ offer secure, overcollateralized Bitcoin lending through a non-custodial 3-of-4 multisig vault structure. It includes built-in arbitration, neutral keyholders, and risk controls — designed for compliant, transparent borrowing.

Q: What is the Bitcoin Treasury Engine™?

A: The Bitcoin Treasury Engine™ is a public company treasury strategy powered by Bitcoin. An ASX-listed entity that accumulates Bitcoin on balance sheet while generating platform revenue — delivering long-term BTC-per-share growth for shareholders and setting a benchmark for sovereign corporate treasuries.

Q: How can I become an Advisor?

A: To become an Advisor, please click on the "Become Advisor" button in the hero section, which will open your email client pre-filled with our contact email: hello@btcvaults.com.

Q: Where can I access the app?

A: You can launch our web application by clicking the "Launch App" button, which will direct you to app.btcvaults.com.
